# mnemonic function, verbatim from the same page
@ AHX {adr}:=A&X&H
@ ALR A:=(A&#{imm})/2
@ ANC A:=A&#{imm}
@ ARR A:=(A&#{imm})/2
@ AXS X:=A&X-#{imm}
@ DCP {adr}:={adr}-1 A-{adr}
@ ISC {adr}:={adr}+1 A:=A-{adr}
@ KIL hangs the processor until reset
@ LAS A,X,S:={adr}&S
@ LAX A,X:={adr}
@ NOP no operation
@ RLA {adr}:={adr}rol A:=A and {adr}
@ RRA {adr}:={adr}ror A:=A adc {adr}
@ SAX {adr}:=A&X
@ SBC A:=A-#{imm}
@ SHX {adr}:=X&H
@ SHY {adr}:=Y&H
@ SLO {adr}:={adr}*2 A:=A or {adr}
@ SRE {adr}:={adr}/2 A:=A exor {adr}
@ TAS S:=A&X {adr}:=S&H
@ XAA A:=X&#{imm}
